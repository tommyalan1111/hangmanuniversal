Privacy

The short version: no account, no email, no tracking pixels. Your game progress stays on your device.

Last updated · 21 November 2026

Hangman Universal is a word game for Android. This page covers the website at hangman-universal-site.web.app and the Android app with package name com.hangmanuniversal.game.

The website

This site is a static introduction to the game. It has no accounts, no forms, no cookies, no analytics and no advertising. It does not host the game and does not store game progress.

The pages are delivered by Firebase Hosting (Google). Google processes standard request information — such as IP address, user agent, requested URL and timestamp — to serve the site, keep it secure and prevent abuse. That processing is covered by Google's privacy policy.

The Android app

The game keeps your level, coins, win streak, solved clues and sound setting in local storage on your device. This data is not sent to us. Uninstalling the app removes it; there is no server-side copy to recover.

The Android build also uses two Google services:

  • Google AdMob — optional banner, interstitial and rewarded ads. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect device identifiers, approximate location, ad interactions and diagnostic data to serve and measure ads. You can limit ad personalization in your device's Google settings.
  • Google Play Billing — used only for the optional one-time “Remove Ads” purchase. Payment details are handled entirely by Google Play; the app never sees your card information.

Third parties

The services above are provided by Google. Their handling of data is described in the Google privacy policy and the Google advertising policies. We do not sell data, and we do not share data with any other third party.

Children

Hangman Universal is a general-audience word game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The website collects no personal information from anyone.

Your choices

  • Uninstall the Android app to remove its local progress.
  • Use your device's ad settings to reset or limit your advertising ID.
  • Clear your browser's site data to remove anything the website stored — it currently stores nothing.

Changes

If this policy changes, the “last updated” date at the top of this page will change too. Material changes will be noted in the project's public repository.

Contact

Questions about privacy, or a request to correct something on this page, can go to the public issue tracker: github.com/tommyalan1111/hangmanuniversal/issues.
